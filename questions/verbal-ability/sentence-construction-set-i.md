I. Directions. Combine the following sentences or phrases and choose the best option among the given sentences.

### 1.

“The sun was setting.”<br>
“The sky turned shades of orange and pink.”

- A. The sun was setting, and the sky turned shades of orange and pink.
- B. The sun was setting, the sky turned shades of orange and pink.
- C. The sun was setting when the sky turned shades of orange and pink.
- D. The sun was setting the sky turned shades of orange and pink.
- E. The sun was setting; the sky turned shades of orange and pink.
