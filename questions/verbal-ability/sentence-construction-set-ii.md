II. Directions. Combine the following sentences or phrases and choose the best option among the given sentences.

### 1.

“The river was overflowing.”<br>
“We had to evacuate the area.”

- A. The river was overflowing; we had to evacuate the area.
- B. Overflowing, the river forced us to evacuate the area.
- C. The river was overflowing, and we had to evacuate the area.
- D. Having overflowed, we had to evacuate the area.
- E. The river was overflowing, we had to evacuate the area.

### 2.

“The event was canceled.”<br>
“There was a sudden storm.”

- A. The event was canceled because there was a sudden storm.
- B. The event was canceled, there was a sudden storm.
- C. If there was a sudden storm, the event was canceled.
- D. There was a sudden storm; hence, the event was canceled.
- E. There was a sudden storm, the event was canceled.

### 3.

“The concert ended.”<br>
“The audience applauded.”

- A. The concert ended, and the audience applauded.
- B. The concert ended, with the audience applauding.
- C. The concert ended; the audience applauded.
- D. The concert ended; the audience, applauding.
- E. The concert ended, the audience applauded.

### 4.

“She not only completed her project.”<br>
“She also presented it at the conference.”

- A. She not only completed her project but also presented it at the conference.
- B. She not only completed her project, she also presented it at the conference.
- C. She not only completed her project, and she also presented it at the conference.
- D. She not only completed her project and also presented it at the conference.
- E. She not only completed her project, she presented it at the conference.

### 5.

“The storm continued.”<br>
“The wind howled.”

- A. The storm continued; the wind howled.
- B. The storm continued, with the wind howling.
- C. The storm continued, and the wind howled.
- D. The storm continued the wind howled.
- E. The storm continued, the wind howled.

### 6.

“The car is a classic model.”<br>
“My grandfather bought it in 1955.”

- A. The car is a classic model, my grandfather bought in 1955.
- B. The classic model car my grandfather bought in 1955.
- C. My grandfather bought the car in 1955, a classic model.
- D. The car is a classic model, bought by my grandfather in 1955.
- E. My grandfather bought the car in 1955, which is a classic model.

### 7.

“She finished her work.”<br>
“She went to the gym.”

- A. She finished her work, and she went to the gym.
- B. After finishing her work, she went to the gym.
- C. She finished her work; then, she went to the gym.
- D. She finished her work, she went to the gym.
- E. She finished her work; going to the gym.

### 8.

“He enjoys painting.”<br>
“He finds it relaxing.”

- A. He enjoys painting, finding it relaxing.
- B. He enjoys painting and finds it relaxing.
- C. Painting, he enjoys and finds it relaxing.
- D. He enjoys, painting and finding it relaxing.
- E. He enjoys painting; he finds it relaxing.

### 9.

“It was raining heavily.”<br>
“They decided to go for a walk.”

- A. Although it was raining heavily, they decided to go for a walk.
- B. It was raining heavily, yet they decided to go for a walk.
- C. They decided to go for a walk although it was raining heavily.
- D. They decided to go for a walk, and it was raining heavily.
- E. They decided to go for a walk despite it was raining heavily.

### 10.

“My brother is an architect.”<br>
“He designed the new city library.”

- A. My brother, an architect, designed the new city library.
- B. My brother is an architect; he designed the new city library.
- C. My brother designed the new city library, an architect.
- D. My brother designed the new city library and is an architect.
- E. My brother is an architect designing the new city library.
