### 1. Read the following sentence and identify the run-on error: "John loves hiking he goes on long trails every weekend."

- A. No run-on error
- B. Comma splice
- C. Missing conjunction
- D. Fragment

### 2. Which of the following sentences correctly fixes the run-on error?

- A. John loves hiking and goes on long trails every weekend.
- B. John loves hiking, goes on long trails every weekend.
- C. John loves hiking; goes on long trails every weekend.
- D. John loves hiking goes on long trails every weekend.

### 3. Identify the run-on sentence in the context of a story:

- A. The rain poured down, and the children played happily in the puddles.
- B. Exhausted from the hike, they decided to rest for a while they enjoyed the beautiful view.
- C. With a loud crash, the thunderstorm startled the birds into flight.
- D. After finishing their meal, they went for a walk around the neighborhood.

### 4. How would you correct this run-on sentence in a business email? "Our meeting is scheduled for 2:00 p.m. I hope everyone can attend."

- A. Our meeting is scheduled for 2:00 p.m. hope everyone can attend.
- B. Our meeting is scheduled for 2:00 p.m.; I hope everyone can attend.
- C. Our meeting is scheduled for 2:00 p.m., I hope everyone can attend.
- D. Our meeting is scheduled for 2:00 p.m., and I hope everyone can attend.

### 5. In a narrative paragraph, identify the run-on sentence:

- A. The detective examined the evidence carefully, he discovered a crucial clue.
- B. As the sun set, the sky turned shades of pink and orange.
- C. Running out of time, she worked quickly to complete the assignment.
- D. The old house creaked in the wind, and the windows rattled.

### 6. Choose the sentence that correctly combines the run-on sentences:

- A. The concert was amazing, the band played all their greatest hits.
- B. The concert was amazing; the band played all their greatest hits.
- C. The concert was amazing the band played all their greatest hits.
- D. The concert was amazing, played all their greatest hits.

### 7. Identify the run-on sentence in a set of instructions:

- A. Mix the ingredients in a bowl until well combined then pour into a baking dish.
- B. Carefully follow the steps to ensure a perfect result.
- C. After stirring the mixture, pour it into the mold.
- D. Make sure to preheat the oven before starting the baking process.

### 8. How would you correct this run-on sentence in a formal essay? "The research study was comprehensive the findings were significant."

- A. The research study was comprehensive, the findings were significant.
- B. The research study was comprehensive and the findings were significant.
- C. The research study was comprehensive; the findings were significant.
- D. The research study was comprehensive, finding significant results.

### 9. In a dialogue between two characters, identify the run-on sentence:

- A. "I can't believe we made it on time," said Maria. "The traffic was terrible."
- B. "I can't believe we made it on time the traffic was terrible," said Maria.
- C. "I can't believe we made it on time," said Maria, "the traffic was terrible."
- D. . "I can't believe we made it on time," said Maria, the traffic was terrible.

### 10. Choose the correctly punctuated sentence to avoid a run-on in a blog post:

- A. Exploring new places is always exciting, it opens up a world of possibilities.
- B. Exploring new places is always exciting and opens up a world of possibilities.
- C. Exploring new places is always exciting; it opens up a world of possibilities.
- D. Exploring new places is always exciting, and it opens up a world of possibilities.
