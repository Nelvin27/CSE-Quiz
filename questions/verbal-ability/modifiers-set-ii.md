II. Directions. Choose the best sentence that uses the participial phrases as a modifier from the sentences in each item.

### 1.

She is eating a lot of apples.<br>
Honey believes that an apple a day keeps the doctor away.

- A. Honey eats a lot of apples, as she believes that an apple a day keeps the doctor away.
- B. Honey eats a lot of apples, as she believes that an apple a day keeps the doctor.
- C. Honey believes that an apple a day keeps the doctor away, hence, she is eating a lot of apples.
- D. Honey believes that an apple a day keeps the doctor, hence, she is eating a lot of apples.

### 2.

I will go to school.<br>
I need to gain more knowledge.

- A. In order to go to school, I need to gain more knowledge.
- B. In order to gain more knowledge, I need to go to school.
- C. Needing to gain more knowledge, I will go to school.
- D. If I go to school, I will gain more knowledge.

### 3.

Anna admired the clear sky.<br>
She is walking through the park.

- A. Walking through the park, Anna admired the clear sky.
- B. Walking through the park, Anna admired the sky.
- C. Anna admired the sky as she was walking through the park.
- D. Anna admired the clear sky as she was walking through the park.

### 4.

The cat is mine.<br>
It is eating a treat.

- A. The cat is mine, eating a treat.
- B. The cat, eating a treat, is mine.
- C. The cat I own, eats a treat.
- D. The cat eating a treat is mine.

### 5.

Tomorrow is another day.<br>
I have to get over this.

- A. Having to get over this, as tomorrow is another.
- B. Having to get over this, tomorrow is another day.
- C. I have to get over this, tomorrow is another day.
- D. I have to get over this as tomorrow is another day.

### 6.

My phone died.<br>
I should definitely buy a new one.

- A. I bought a new phone, but my phone died.
- B. I bought a new phone, definitely a new one.
- C. As my phone is dying, I should definitely buy a new one.
- D. Given that my phone died, I should definitely buy a new one.

### 7.

I love myself.<br>
I will buy everything

- A. I will buy everything, as I love myself.
- B. I will buy it all, as I love myself.
- C. Loving myself, I will buy everything.
- D. Loving myself, I will buy it all.

### 8.

Social media is made to aid communication.<br>
It is causing addiction nowadays.

- A. Social media, intended to aid communication, is causing addiction nowadays.
- B. Social media is intended for communication, yet it is causing addiction nowadays.
- C. Social media is causing addiction nowadays, yet it is intended to aid communication.
- D. Social media is causing addiction nowadays and is causing addiction nowadays.

### 9.

He is riding a bike around the city.<br>
Caleb ignored all his phone notifications.

- A. Caleb ignored his notifications, riding a bike around the city.
- B. Caleb ignored his notifications, as he is riding a bike around the city.
- C. Riding a bike around the city, Caleb ignored his notifications.
- D. Riding a bike around the city, Caleb ignored all his phone notifications.

### 10.

He is going to sleep early.<br>
Andy has a morning class.

- A. Andy sleeps early, for his morning class.
- B. Going to sleep early, Andy is preparing for his morning class.
- C. Going to a morning class, Andy sleeps early.
- D. Andy has a morning class, he is going to sleep early.
