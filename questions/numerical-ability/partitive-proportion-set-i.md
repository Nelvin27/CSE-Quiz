# Partitive Proportion Set I

Read the following problems and solve for what is asked. Choose the letter of the correct answer.

### 1. A farm has a total for 345 livestock, there are chicken, pigs, and ducks in a ratio of 7:3:5. How many pigs are there?

- A. 161
- B. 69
- C. 115
- D. 207

### 2. A total of ₱10,000 is to be divided among Greg, Darwin, and Marie in a ratio of 2:3:5. How much does Darwin gets?

- A. ₱2,000
- B. ₱3,000
- C. ₱5,000
- D. ₱4,000

### 3. The total weight of the Annie, Rico, and Jasmine is 115 kg. Their weight ratio is 3:1:4. How much heavier is Annie than Rico?

- A. 43.13 kg
- B. 14.38 kg
- C. 57.5 kg
- D. 28.75 kg

### 4. A wire measuring 23 meters for electrical installation and is going to be cut into three pieces with a ratio of 2:1:5. How long is the shortest wire?

- A. 2.88 m
- B. 5.75 m
- C. 11.5 m
- D. 14.38 m

### 5. A triangle with a perimeter of 231 cm has a ratio of 2:3:2. What is the length of the longest side?

- A. 99 cm
- B. 66 cm
- C. 33 cm
- D. 132 cm

### 6. The election result produced a plurality among three candidates. If the total votes of 1580 had a ratio of 9:7:4, how many votes did the winning candidate receive?

- A. 553 votes
- B. 711 votes
- C. 316 votes
- D. 395 votes

### 7. The ratio of Narra to Talisay to Coconuts in the tree nursery is 3:4:5. If there are a total of 1032 tree saplings, how many of them are Narra?

- A. 344
- B. 430
- C. 172
- D. 258

### 8. Ms. Colas has budgeted her monthly utility expenses for internet, electricity, and water into a 1:4:2 ratio. If her utility bill for the month is ₱9,548, how much did she pay for her electricity?

- A. ₱1,364
- B. ₱2,728
- C. ₱4,092
- D. ₱5,456

- A. 68.75 mL

- C. 137.5 mL
