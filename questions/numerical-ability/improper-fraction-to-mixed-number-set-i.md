Multiple Choice. Choose the correct answer.

[Original Google Form](https://forms.gle/7vw4fgwnabPVjr3t5)

### 1. Which of the following is equivalent to 9/2?

- A. 2 1/9
- B. 2 1/4
- C. 1 2/4
- D. 4 1/2

### 2. Which of the following is equivalent to 87/7?

- A. 7 4/12
- B. 4 7/12
- C. 12 3/7
- D. 7 1/3

- A. 9 4/37
- B. 37 4/9
