### 1. A ladder is leaning against a wall. The base of the ladder is 6 feet away from the wall, and it reaches a height of 8 feet on the wall. What is the length of the ladder?

- A. 10 feet
- B. 12 feet
- C. 14 feet
- D. 8 feet

### 2. A rectangular swimming pool measures 15 meters long and 10 meters wide. If you want to build a deck that is uniformly wide around the pool, increasing its total area to 250\\m², what will be the length of the deck?

- A. 5 meters
- B. 25 meters
- C. 10 meters
- D. 15 meters

### 3. A triangle has a perimeter of 90. If 2 of its sides are equal and the third side is 6 more than the equal sides, what is the length of the third side?

- A. 30
- B. 34
- C. 38
- D. 42

### 4. The length of a rectangle is 15 cm less than 3 times its width. If the parameter of the rectangle is 160 cm, find the length and the width of the rectangle.

- A. l = 53.25 cm and w = 19.75 cm
- B. l = 54.25 cm and w = 20.75 cm
- C. l = 55.25 cm and w = 22.75 cm
- D. l = 56.25 cm and w = 23.75 cm

### 5. The length of a playing field is 9ft less than twice its width. If the parameter of the field is 81ft, find the length and the width of the field.

- A. l = 24 ft and w = 16.5 ft
- B. l = 14.5 ft and w = 22 ft
- C. l = 16.5 ft and w = 23 ft
- D. l = 18.5 ft and w = 26 ft

### 6. Princess ran around a rectangular field whose length was 40 meters and width was 25 meters. If she ran around 3 times, how far did she run?

- A. 330 meters
- B. 360 meters
- C. 390 meters
- D. 420 meter

### 7. A parallelogram has a base of 21 inches and a height of 7 inches. What is the area of the parallelogram?

- A. 144 square inches
- B. 147 square inches
- C. 150 square inches
- D. 156 square inches

### 8. Pearl plans to paint her rectangular living room, which has a length of 24 feet and a width of 18 feet. What is the length of the diagonal of her living room in feet?

- A. 25 feet
- B. 30 feet
- C. 35 feet
- D. 40 feet

### 9. In a rectangular parking lot, a walking path is built along the perimeter. The length of the park is 60 meters, and the width is 40 meters. If the path is 10 meters wide, what is the area of the path in square meters?

- A. 1200 square meters
- B. 2000 square meters
- C. 2400 square meters
- D. 2600 square meters
