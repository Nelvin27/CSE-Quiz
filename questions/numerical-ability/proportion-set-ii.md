### 1. A recipe calls for 2 cups of flour to make 12 cookies. If you want to make 36 cookies, how much flour do you need?

- A. 4
- B. 5
- C. 6
- D. 7

### 2. A car travels 240 kilometers using 15 liters of gas. If the car's gas tank has a capacity of 35 liters, how far can the car travel on a full tank?

- A. 560 km
- B. 480 km
- C. 880 km
- D. 360 km

### 3. It takes 4 hours for 3 painters to paint a wall. How long would it take 6 painters to paint the same wall?

- A. 2 hours
- B. 1.5 hours
- C. 1 hour
- D. 30 minutes

### 4. A person needs 60 grams of protein per day. If they consume 45 grams of protein, what percentage of their daily requirement have they fulfilled?

- A. 60%
- B. 75%
- C. 50%
- D. 80%

### 5. If a train travels at a speed of 60 kph, how long will it take to cover a distance of 180 kilometers?

- A. 1.5 hours
- B. 6 hours
- C. 5 hours
- D. 3 hours

### 6. If a discount store offers a 20% discount on all items, how much money can you save on a ₱5,500 purchase?

- A. ₱4,400
- B. ₱3,300
- C. ₱2,200
- D. ₱1,100

### 7. A recipe requires 2 hours of cooking time for 1 kg of meat. How long should the meat cook if you have 1.5 kg?

- A. 3 hours
- B. 3.5 hours
- C. 4 hours
- D. 4.5 hours

### 8. On a scale drawing, 1 inch represents 5 feet. If a room is 25 feet long, what is its length on the scale drawing?

- A. 10 inches
- B. 5 inches
- C. 15 inches

### 9. Two workers can complete a project in 10 days. How many days will it take for five workers to complete the same project?

- A. 6 days
- B. 8 days
