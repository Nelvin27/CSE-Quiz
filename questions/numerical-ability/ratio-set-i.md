# Ratio Set I

Read and solve each of the problems below. Choose the letter of the correct answer.

[Original Google Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfu7vHjfxF6AUTsCf4AOOO_FM9elMBbl2_1YDYdCYvwFcm68g/viewform)

### 1. In a cake recipe, the ratio of flour to sugar is 3:2. If you need 6 cups of sugar, how much flour do you need?

- A. 6
- B. 7
- C. 8
- D. 9

### 2. In a class of 30 students, the ratio of boys to girls is 2:3. How many boys are in the class?

- A. 12
- B. 18
- C. 14
- D. 16

### 3. To create a shade of purple, the ratio of blue paint to red paint is 5:2. If you have 35 liters of blue paint, how much red paint do you need?

- A. 14
- B. 15
- C. 16
- D. 17

### 4. A sum of $600 is divided among three friends in the ratio 2:3:5. How much money does each friend receive?

- A. 160, 160, 280
- B. 120, 170, 310
- C. 120, 180, 300
- D. 100, 200, 300

### 5. The ratio of apples to oranges in a fruit basket is 4:3. If there are 27 oranges, how many apples are there?

- A. 35
- B. 36
- C. 37
- D. 38

### 6. Two workers can complete a task in the ratio of 4:5. If the first worker takes 8 hours to complete the task, how many hours does the second worker take?

- A. 10
- B. 11
- C. 12
- D. 13

### 7. The ratio of flower seeds to vegetable seeds in a packet is 3:2. If there are 150 flower seeds, how many vegetable seeds are there?

- A. 50
- B. 100
- C. 70
- D. 90

### 8. In a class, the ratio of students who passed and failed is 3:2. The passing rate is 80%. If there are 10 students who failed, how many students passed?

- A. 14
- B. 15
- C. 16
- D. 17

### 9. A car travels 240 kilometers in 4 hours. What is the ratio of the distance traveled to the time taken?

- A. 50 km/h
- B. 70 km/h
- C. 40 km/h
- D. 60 km/h

### 10. The sum of the ages of two friends is 45. If their ages are in the ratio 3:2, what are their ages?

- A. 27 &amp; 18
- B. 15 &amp; 30
- C. 25 &amp; 20
- D. 23 &amp; 22
