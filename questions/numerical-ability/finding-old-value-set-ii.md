II. Read the following word problems and solve for what is asked. Round off your answers to two decimal places, if any. Select the letter of the correct answer

### 1. Maria schedules their family’s grocery shopping on a weekend. For the current week, she noticed that she paid 20% more for 5 kg of rice amounting to ₱350. How much was the same amount of rice a week before?

- A. 255.20
- B. 291.67
- C. 305.33
- D. 282.22

### 2. Juan rides a bus on his way to work and just recently, it has increased their fare by 25% due to increasing fuel prices. If he is paying ₱50 for a one-way ticket, what was the original fare?

- A. 40
- B. 30
- C. 50
- D. 20

### 3. Emily answered 80% of the questions correctly on a math quiz. If she got 32 questions right, how many questions were on the quiz in total?

- A. 70
- B. 60
- C. 50
- D. 40

### 4. A research team testing the efficacy of their new product determined that 15% of the bacteria survived after an exposure to a certain treatment. If the number of surviving bacteria is 90, what was the initial number of bacteria before the treatment?

- A. 654.65
- B. 710.24
- C. 575.25
- D. 600.00

### 5. During the Tacloban City book fair, Dee bought a first edition hardbound copy of a book for only ₱1500 at a 25% discount. What was the original cost of the book?

- A. ₱2300.11
- B. ₱1900.00
- C. ₱2000.00
- D. ₱2100.25

### 6. As part of their opening promo, a tech store offered a 15% discount on their laptops. Kaleb bought a laptop for ₱42,500 during this period. What was the original cost of the laptop?

- A. ₱50,000
- B. ₱55,000
- C. ₱60,000
- D. ₱55,000

### 7. As an adjustment to the high inflation, the monthly rent for Austin’s apartment increased by 10%. If the new monthly cost is ₱16,500, what was the original monthly rent?

- A. ₱15,000
- B. ₱15,500
- C. ₱14,000
- D. ₱14,500

### 8. In the geography category of a game show, 30% of the participants correctly identified notable places around the world. If 45 participants got it right, how many people were in the show?

- A. 110
- B. 150
- C. 170
- D. 100

### 9. Nikko has been going to the same gym for almost 5 years and as a loyalty reward, the gym offered a 30% discount on his monthly subscription. If the new monthly fee is ₱560, what was the original monthly fee?

- A. ₱700
- B. ₱600
- C. ₱900
- D. ₱800

### 10. Sandra has an upcoming overseas trip with her friends in the next month. Due to unexpected changes, her budget rose by 12% which now amounts to ₱22,400. What was her original travel budget?

- C. ₱22,000
