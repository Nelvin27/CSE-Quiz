### 1. Maurice left Sogod traveling 52 mph. Vince, to catch up, left some time later driving at 62 mph. Vince caught up after 6 hours. How long was Maurice driving before Vince caught up?

- A. 7 hours and 9 minutes
- B. 7 hours and 12 minutes
- C. 7 hours and 15 minutes
- D. 7 hours and 18 minutes

### 2. Lea left Cebu City traveling at 50 mph, while, at the same time, Aleiah left the city going the opposite direction at a speed of 40 mph. Find the time Lea traveled before the two were 180 miles apart.

- A. 1.5 hours
- B. 2 hours
- C. 2.5 hours
- D. 3 hours

### 3. A J&amp;T cargo plane flew from Manila across the South Luzon region at 261 mph and flew back to Manila at 281 mph. Given that the first trip took two hours longer, how long was the return trip?

- A. 1.80 hours
- B. 1.86 hours
- C. 1. 94 hours
- D. 2 hours

### 4. Paul left downtown Tacloban, and three hours later, Selena left going 66 mph to catch up with Paul. After another two hours, Selena caught up. Find Paul’s average speed.

- A. 26.1 hours
- B. 26.2 hours
- C. 26.3 hours
- D. 26.4 hours

### 5. Jameboy left San Miguel with a speed of 49 mph. Raymund also left at the same time in the opposite direction at a speed of 51 mph. Find how many hours they must travel before they are 200 miles apart.

- A. 1.5 hours
- B. 2 hours
- C. 2.5 hours
- D. 3 hours

### 6. Sara traveled to Basey by car. Going there took 5 hours, and the return trip lasted 4 hours. Sara averaged a speed of 50 mph while returning. Find the average speed of the trip there.

- A. 30 mph
- B. 40 mph
- C. 50 mph
- D. 60 mph

### 7. Alfred left the city for vacation. Benny left 8 hours later going 40 mph to catch up with Alfred. After 8 hours Benny caught up. What was Alfred’s average speed?

- A. 20 mph
- B. 40 mph
- C. 50 mph
- D. 70 mph

### 8. A plane set off to Bangkok at a speed of 300 mph. On the return flight of 3 hours, the plane cruised at 350 mph. How many hours long was the flight to Bangkok?

- A. 3 hours and 30 minutes
- B. 3 hours and 40 minutes
- C. 3 hours and 50 minutes
- D. 4 hours

### 9. A train left for P. Burgos station, and 30 minutes later, a car traveling 45 mph tried catching up to the train. After 15 minutes, the car caught up. What was the train's average speed?

- A. 10.5 mph
- B. 11 mph
- C. 13.5 mph
- D. 15 mph

### 10. A jeepney and motorcycle left from San Jose in opposite directions at the same time. The jeepney traveled at 45 mph. The vehicles were 50 miles apart after 30 mins. Find the motorcycle’s average speed.
