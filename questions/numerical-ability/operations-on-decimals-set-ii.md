# Operations on Decimals Set II

Solve the following word problems. Round off the answer to two decimal places. Choose the letter of the correct answer.

[Original Google Form](https://forms.gle/x9MLmJhgntbcDbDb7)

### 1. You buy a dress for ₱2,678.75 and shoes for ₱5,378.50. How much did you spend in total?

- A. ₱8,057.25
- B. ₱8,057.00
- C. ₱8,157.25
- D. ₱8,075.15

### 2. A recipe calls for 2.5 cups of flour and 1.75 cups of sugar. How much flour and sugar do you need in total?

- A. 4.20
- B. 4.25
- C. 4.30
- D. 4.15

### 3. Amy has $289.60 in her bank account and deposited $67.45. On the same day, she made a withdrawal of $100.00. What is her new balance?

- A. $257.15
- B. $257.05
- C. $275.05
- D. $257.45

### 4. Hannah budgeted ₱20,000 for a trip, and she spent a total of ₱18,143.25. However, she missed her flight back home and had to rebook a ticket using her credit card for ₱4,362.33. How much money did she spend over the budget?

- A. – ₱2,505.58
- B. ₱2,500.58
- C. – ₱2,500.58
- D. ₱2,505.58

### 5. Valentin has 17.90 kg of rice and he used 10 cups to cook for a family event. If a cup of rice is equivalent to 256.75 g, how many kilograms of rice does he have left?

- A. 12.16 kg
- B. 11.11 kg
- C. 16.00 kg
- D. 15.33 kg

### 6. A tree grew by 3.37 meters, but then it was pruned and lost 1.2 meters. What is the new height of the tree?

- A. 2.07
- B. 2.27
- C. 2.17
- D. 2.37

### 7. Camilla created a garden with a length of 8.55 meters and a width of 5.2 meters. What is the area of the garden?

- A. 40.66
- B. 49.36
- C. 44.46
- D. 54.75

### 8. The coat costs ₱3,499.75, and there is a 15% discount. What is the discounted price?

- A. ₱2,974.79
- B. ₱524.96
- C. ₱3,484.74
- D. ₱4,024.71

### 9. You and your friends ordered 3 pizzas, and each pizza has 8 slices. How many slices does each person get if there are 5 of you?

- A. 4.00
- B. 4.80
- C. 5.20
- D. 4.99

- A. 16.37
